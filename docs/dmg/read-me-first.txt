NFS Hot Pursuit 2 for Mac
=========================

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 15 or later, and your own disc image (.iso) of
Need for Speed: Hot Pursuit 2 for the PlayStation 2, USA (SLUS-20362).

1. Drag "NFS Hot Pursuit 2 Installer" to Applications and open it.
   The first time, macOS blocks it (it isn't signed by Apple): open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the installer, then confirm.
2. If macOS asks for Apple's Command Line Tools, click Install. The installer waits and goes on by itself.
3. Choose your disc image. The installer copies the game's files (2.9 GB) to
   ~/Library/Application Support/NFS Hot Pursuit 2, translates the game's program to C++ and compiles it on
   your Mac (a few minutes, once).
4. "NFS Hot Pursuit 2" is put in Applications and opens. Play it from there from now on.

This DMG holds no game code and no game data. Details, credits and the source: https://github.com/sanyokkua/nfs-hp2-ps2-port
